render

Pick circle or draw (C / D keys), drag on either image to mark a spot, then type what is wrong there. Notes save on their own to renders/notes/<render>.json. Delete removes the selected mark. Each mark has a thread: the first message is the note, then reply under it (Ctrl/Cmd+Enter posts). Green marks are ones an agent says are ready to look at again: tick fixed, or reply and send them back.
